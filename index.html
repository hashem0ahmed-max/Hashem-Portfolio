Chapter 03 The tech Snoonu · Doha · 2025–26

Snoonu · Brand Manager · 2025–26

Snoonu was a food delivery app. I made people believe it was a super app.

A company-wide value proposition, new brand guidelines and one voice across every vertical. Then four brand campaigns to prove it. Along the way: two e-sports tournaments, partnerships with Qatar Foundation and Qatar TV, and a Rising Star Award in my first three months.

FOODGROCERYMARKETPLACECRMIN-APP

What was the Snoonu Hackathon?

Qatar's largest hackathon, built from a duo's will to drive impact.

Snoonu is Qatar's homegrown super app. In 2026 we invited developers, designers and students to build on real Snoonu challenges. 750 people signed up, including teams from abroad, and it trended nationally. Two of us conceived it, pitched it and ran it end to end: the idea, the brand, the partners, and the story told before, during and after.

04 · Hired20hired straight into Snoonu
